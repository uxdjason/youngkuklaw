[108] BATTERY.  

COLE versus TURNER. Pasch. 3 Ann.  

6 Mod. 149.  

At Nisi Prius, upon evidence in trespass for assault and battery, Holt C.J.  declared, 1. That the least touching of another in anger is a battery. 2. If two or  more meet in a narrow passage, arid without any violence or design of harm, the one  touches the other gently, it is no battery. 3. If any of them use violence against the  other, to force his way in a rude inordinate manner, it is a battery ; or any struggle  about the passage, to that degree as may do hurt, is a battery. Vid. Bro. Tresp. 236.  7 E. 4, 36.  22 Ass. 60.  3 H. 4, 9.  

Note; It was in action of battery by husband and wife, for a battery upon the  husband and wife, ad dampnum ipsorum ; and though the plaintiff had a verdict, yet  the Chief Justice said, he should never have judgment. And judgment was after  arrested above upon that exception.
